GOOD TO KNOW

Before you

Are these fixed prices? +

They are starting prices for clearly scoped work. We confirm exact deliverables and final price after understanding your requirements.

Do I pay separately for tools? +

Yes, unless your proposal explicitly says otherwise. Platforms, hosting, domains, premium SEO tools, paid plugins, ad spend, and API usage are typically billed directly to you or itemized separately. We recommend tools based on need rather than adding subscriptions you do not need.

Can we pay online later? +

Yes. This page is structured for future checkout. Stripe or Paystack checkout is not active yet; payment terms and an approved invoice or payment link will be confirmed before payment.

Can you combine services? +

Absolutely. Packages are starting points. We can scope a custom engagement around the mix of design, development, SEO, paid media, creative, analytics, and automation your team needs.
